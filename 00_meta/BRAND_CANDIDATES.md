# Brand naming method, then names

Cover stays **Name pending**. Nothing here is selected, registered, or cleared. A web search miss is not a trademark clearance.

## What famous lock names actually come from

| Name | Source type | What the record says |
|---|---|---|
| Yale | Founder surname | Linus Yale. The company took the inventor's family name. |
| Schlage | Founder surname | Walter Schlage. The company took his name. |
| Kwikset | A real install benefit, spelled as it sounds | Secondary retellings say the first tubular locks were fast to set, so the name is "quick-set". This pass did not read the company archive. |
| ASSA | Founder initials | August Stenman. The letters are his name, rearranged. |
| Abloy | Legal name, shortened | Swedish and Finnish for a lock-factory company, contracted. |
| dormakaba | Two company names joined | The joined name is the 2015 merger of Dorma and Kaba. A secondary history says Dorma comes from two surnames and Kaba from an older cash-register word. That older split was not read from the company archive. |
| Allegion | Agency coinage from real English roots | Lippincott tied it to allegiance and protection. The root is hearable. It is not a shared fake ending. |
| Nuki | Benefit coinage, changed after a collision | It began as Noki, "no key". Nokia objected. It became Nuki, explained as "new key". The check happened almost too late. |

August is a known retrofit lock company. This pass did not find a reliable explanation of why that word was chosen, so it is not used as a rule.

## Rules for this product

1. Do not use a founder surname. No person has been chosen to put on the brand.
2. Do not name an unverified benefit. Quick, universal fit, and "original key always remains" are not allowed in the name.
3. Do not put Lock, Smart, Retrofit, or a certification mark in the brand word.
4. Company name, brand, series, and model stay separate. A Chinese company-name candidate is not the English brand.
5. For installers, the word must be sayable once and spellable from hearing. One shortlist must not share one ending.
6. Before any name is sent, check exact spelling, sound-alikes, and famous neighbors. Noki is the warning. A quiet web search is not that check.
7. The first three names failed rule 5. The six names suggested before this study are not the shortlist.
