01. Is this a full lock replacement or a retrofit?
One drive core, two interior retrofit paths. Not a claim that every door can take it.

02. What are the two product paths?
S1 keeps the existing cylinder. S2 replaces a Euro cylinder.

03. Are the English and Chinese catalogues two products?
No. They are two language versions of the same catalogue.

Do not promise that it always remains. The keep-cylinder path says the original key stays. The replace-cylinder path says matching keys are included. Check the door.
